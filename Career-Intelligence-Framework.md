# Career Intelligence Framework

### An Evidence-Based Learning Framework for Career Exploration, Career Transition, and Workforce Readiness

> Helping people make informed career decisions through self-assessment, labour market intelligence, workforce analytics, and authentic learning.

---

# Why this initiative?

Career decisions influence education, employability, lifelong learning, and career satisfaction.

Yet many people make those decisions based on limited information, external pressure, or incomplete knowledge of occupations and labour market opportunities.

The Career Intelligence Framework was independently developed to help learners make better career decisions by combining personal strengths with objective occupational and labour market evidence.

Originally created during the COVID-19 pandemic, the framework has been continuously updated to reflect changing occupations, emerging technologies, workforce trends, and evolving employer expectations.

---

# The Learning Journey

Rather than asking learners to simply choose a career, the framework guides them through a structured decision-making process.

```text
Understand Yourself
        ↓
Explore Occupations
        ↓
Analyse Labour Market Intelligence
        ↓
Compare Career Options
        ↓
Evaluate the Evidence
        ↓
Make an Informed Decision
        ↓
Create a Career Development Plan
```

Each stage builds confidence through investigation, comparison, reflection, and practical application.

---

# What Makes This Different?

Instead of asking:

> **"What career sounds interesting?"**

participants investigate:

> **"What evidence supports this career choice?"**

Learning activities combine:

- Personal self-assessment
- Occupational data
- Labour market intelligence
- Data visualisation
- Guided reflection
- Authentic learner deliverables

The result is a learning experience that develops judgement rather than simply providing information.

---

# Framework Components

| Component | Purpose |
|-----------|----------|
| Self-Assessment | Understand interests, preferences, and work styles |
| Career Exploration | Discover occupations using structured evidence |
| Occupational Profiles | Analyse work activities, technologies, and workplace requirements |
| Labour Market Intelligence | Explore demand, future outlook, and employment trends |
| Visual Analytics | Compare personal profiles with occupational characteristics |
| Reflection Activities | Evaluate alternatives and justify decisions |
| Career Planning | Produce an evidence-based career development plan |

---

# Learning Design Principles

The framework integrates several complementary disciplines.

- Instructional Design
- Career Development Theory
- Workforce Analytics
- Labour Market Intelligence
- Learning Analytics
- Data Visualisation
- Digital Learning (MoodleCloud)

Participants actively investigate, compare, analyse, and reflect rather than passively consuming information.

---

# Who Can Benefit?

The framework is suitable for:

- Secondary and higher education
- University applicants
- Career exploration programmes
- Career transition and reskilling initiatives
- Employability programmes
- Workforce development
- Adult learning
- Career coaching
- Organisations supporting employee career development

The same design philosophy underpins my AI Enablement Framework, where workplace AI adoption begins with analysing work tasks before selecting AI solutions.
