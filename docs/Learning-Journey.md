# Learning Journey

### From Self-Discovery to Evidence-Based Career Decisions

---

# Overview

The Career Intelligence Lab was designed as an active learning experience rather than a collection of career information.

Participants investigate their own strengths, explore occupations using multiple sources of evidence, compare alternatives, and produce practical career decisions supported by data.

Each activity builds upon the previous one, gradually developing confidence, career awareness, and informed decision-making.

---

# The Learning Journey

```text
Self Assessment
        ↓
Career Interests
        ↓
Explore Occupations
        ↓
Compare Career Intelligence Reports
        ↓
Analyse Labour Market Evidence
        ↓
Identify Skills & Knowledge Gaps
        ↓
Evaluate Career Alternatives
        ↓
Develop a Personal Career Plan
```

The journey encourages learners to investigate, compare, reflect, and justify their decisions rather than simply selecting a career from a list.

---

# Typical Learning Activities

Participants complete authentic learning tasks such as:

- Completing a career interest self-assessment
- Exploring occupations using Career Intelligence Reports
- Comparing multiple career pathways
- Analysing labour market trends
- Identifying transferable skills
- Investigating emerging technologies
- Evaluating future employment opportunities
- Reflecting on personal strengths and preferences
- Developing an evidence-based career development plan

---

# Learning by Discovery

Rather than providing answers, the framework encourages learners to ask meaningful questions throughout the learning process.

Examples include:

- Which occupations best match my strengths?
- What evidence supports that conclusion?
- Which skills are employers seeking?
- Which occupations are growing?
- What technologies should I begin learning?
- What realistic pathways can help me reach my career goals?

The emphasis is on developing informed judgement rather than simply finding a "right" answer.

---

# Authentic Learner Deliverables

Throughout the learning experience, participants create practical outputs that document their thinking and support future career planning.

Examples include:

- Personal RIASEC profile
- Career comparison tables
- Occupational research summaries
- Skills gap analysis
- Career evidence portfolio
- Personal development plan
- Career action plan

These deliverables encourage reflection, critical thinking, and evidence-based decision-making.

---

# Learning Outcomes

By the end of the learning journey participants are able to:

- Understand their interests and work preferences
- Explore occupations using multiple sources of evidence
- Interpret visual career analytics
- Compare career alternatives objectively
- Identify future learning priorities
- Recognise transferable skills
- Make informed career decisions
- Develop a realistic career development plan

---

# Professional Value

The learning journey reflects my facilitation approach.

Rather than relying on lectures or static career information, participants actively explore, analyse, compare, discuss, and reflect.

The result is a learner-centred experience that promotes confidence, autonomy, and practical decision-making.
